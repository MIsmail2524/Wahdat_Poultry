Company

Two decades of better eggs.

Founded in 2006, Wahdat set out to give Pakistan something it didn't have: a branded, enriched, consistently fresh egg. Today we are a listed, vertically integrated producer preparing for global markets.

Our purpose

Pure, enriched and internationally certified eggs for a healthier Pakistan — and a healthier region.

Mission

Put a nutritionally enriched, traceable egg on every shelf — at a price families can afford.

Vision

Become South Asia's most trusted egg exporter and the reference brand for enriched eggs in the Gulf.

Values

Food safety first · animal welfare · transparency with buyers and shareholders · relentless freshness.

Milestones

How we got here.

2006

Founded

Wahdat begins egg production with a focus on hygiene and modern housing.

Early

Farm Fresh Eggs brand

Pioneers branded, packaged and enriched eggs in Pakistan — Omega-3, Golden and Classic lines.

FY21

Rs1.23bn revenue

Automated layer farms and in-house packaging scale distribution across major cities.

FY25

Rs2.79bn revenue

Four automated farms, ~430,000 birds, up to 400,000 eggs a day and 1,500+ outlets. Profit after tax Rs241.9m.

2026

PSX listing

IPO oversubscribed 5.2× in book-building, raising Rs956m. Expansion of flock and a liquid-egg plant begins.
