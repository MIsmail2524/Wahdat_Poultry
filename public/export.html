Frequently asked

What is the minimum order?

Programmes are usually planned in full reefer loads. Trial lots for new buyers are discussed case by case with the export desk.

Can you pack under our brand?

Yes — private-label trays and cartons can be printed in English and Arabic to your artwork.

How do I verify a shipment?

Each export lot carries a batch code. Enter it on our traceability page to see farm, lay date and QC result.

When will liquid egg be available?

The pasteurisation plant is being built with IPO proceeds. Register interest through the RFQ form and we will contact you when commercial runs begin.
